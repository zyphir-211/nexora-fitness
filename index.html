RECOVERY

Rest-day ideas

Rest is a normal part of a training routine. Optional activities should stay easy and comfortable.

  • Take an easy walk if it feels good.
  • Try gentle mobility without forcing a stretch.
  • Get regular meals, water and sleep.
  • Skip activity when you need extra rest.
