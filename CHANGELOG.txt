NEXORA FITNESS — RELEASE HISTORY
================================

Use the in-app What’s New page (More → What’s new, or Browse → Personalization → What’s new) to search releases by version, feature or fix. This file is the portable copy of the same history.

- Smart weekly planner with preferred training-day suggestions.
- Schedule saved custom routines without replacing the weekly plan.
- Session Recovery card with local checkpoints and safe discard.
- Training Workspace with exercise/set summary, preview and Planner shortcut.
- Searchable in-app Update History.

9.8.3 — Appearance options
- Expanded colour choices and controlled appearance settings.
- Additional spacing, card, corner, background, button, navigation and motion options.

9.8.2 — Interaction update
- NEXORA logo returns to Today.
- First-workout prompt opens the Planner.
- Dashboard shortcuts and internal links route to their intended sections.

9.8.1 — Navigation hotfix
- Restored floating bottom navigation.
- Positioned More menu below its trigger and above page content.

9.8 Beta — Fusion redesign
- New Today, Train, Progress and Browse layouts.
- Responsive navigation, light/dark modes and updated cards and charts.

9.7.0 — NEXORA identity and settings refinement
- NEXORA branding and heart-and-pulse identity.
- Simplified dashboard, colour-named themes and restored familiar Settings.

9.6.2 — Today and Browse navigation
- Today focus metrics and searchable Browse directory.

9.5.0 — Customization Studio

9.4.1 — Appearance reliability fixes

9.4.0 — Appearance overhaul
- Refined NEXORA visual identity, cards, navigation and appearance themes.

9.3.0 — Training Flow

9.2.0 — Settings and smoothness

9.1.0 — Design and accessibility
- Visual system refinements, keyboard navigation, focus states, screen-reader improvements and responsive fixes.

9.0.0 — Personalization
- Personal profile, dashboard preferences, planner preferences, achievements and training insights.
